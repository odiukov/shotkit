# Genre engines (reference)

The native reversal shapes of vertical short drama. Each engine is a **premise + a payoff cadence the audience is buying** — pick one as the primary, then run the hook / reversal-cadence / cliffhanger rules from SKILL.md on top. The engine tells you *what* the turns are made of; SKILL.md tells you *where* they land.

**Engines stack.** Real hits braid two or three — reborn + revenge, secret-billionaire + revenge, contract-marriage + substitute-bride. Take one as the primary (it owns the spine and the cliffhanger shape) and graft a second's signature beats onto it. Don't braid three unless each is still legible in one line.

---

## Revenge glow-up (逆袭)

- **Premise** — a humiliated, betrayed protagonist returns transformed (rich, beautiful, powerful) to dismantle the people who wronged them. The fantasy is cathartic, unanswerable payback.
- **Hook** — the betrayal or rock-bottom moment, up front. Establish the wound in seconds or every later payoff reads as unearned.
- **Beat rhythm** — humiliation (or its flashback) → glow-up reveal → a **targeted takedown of ONE named antagonist per episode**. A "face-slap", literal or figurative, lands every episode.
- **Cliffhanger** — the villain about to be exposed; the new status revealed to an old tormentor; a counter-scheme launching. Cut on the verge of the reversal.
- **Emotional engine** — catharsis, justice served.
- **Avoid** — diffuse revenge. Every episode needs a named target and a win; a slow burn with no per-episode payoff is where viewers leave.

## Reborn / rewind (重生)

- **Premise** — betrayed or killed, the heroine wakes up back in time and weaponises foreknowledge against a disaster she has already lived through.
- **Hook** — the death / ruin itself, played straight, then the snap back to "before". The audience must feel the loss before they enjoy the do-over.
- **Beat rhythm** — disaster → rewind → she acts on knowledge no one else has (and everyone reads it as madness) → the known catastrophe arrives and she is three steps ahead → reversal of a beat we watched go wrong the first time.
- **Cliffhanger** — a detail that did NOT happen the first time (the timeline is diverging), or the villain showing knowledge he shouldn't have.
- **Emotional engine** — dramatic irony. The pleasure is that *we* know too, and no one on screen does.
- **Avoid** — foreknowledge that makes her invincible. Keep her power partial: the timeline pushes back, and something she "remembers" turns out to be wrong.

## Secret identity / secret billionaire (霸总)

- **Premise** — the disrespected nobody is secretly the most powerful person in the room. Every slight is a debt the audience knows will be collected.
- **Hook** — the disrespect, at maximum. Someone spits on a man who could buy the building.
- **Beat rhythm** — humiliation of the "nobody" → a hint only the audience catches (a deferential call, a ring, a name dropped) → escalating misreadings by the antagonist → the reveal, staged for maximum room-silencing effect.
- **Cliffhanger** — the reveal half-landed: the antagonist has just learned who he insulted and the consequence hasn't hit yet.
- **Emotional engine** — anticipated status inversion. The wait *is* the product; don't rush the reveal.
- **Avoid** — revealing too early, and a lead who is smug. He must absorb the insult with dignity, or catharsis turns to arrogance.

## Contract marriage

- **Premise** — two people marry for a deal — money, a visa, an inheritance, a family obligation — and fall in love after. The cold one falls first, and hides it worst.
- **Hook** — the terms, stated coldly, by two people who mean them. The colder the contract, the better the payoff.
- **Beat rhythm** — the deal → forced proximity (one bed, one house, one public appearance) → a crack in the cold one's armour that only the audience sees → a jealousy or protection beat that breaks the contract's logic → the terms get renegotiated by feeling instead of money.
- **Cliffhanger** — one of them about to discover the other's real motive, or the contract's end date arriving early.
- **Emotional engine** — the melt. Status flips from transaction to need.
- **Avoid** — warmth too early. The contract must be genuinely, uncomfortably cold, or there's nothing to thaw.

## Substitute bride

- **Premise** — an overlooked stand-in takes the real bride's place and wins the groom, the family, and the room the entitled original assumed were hers.
- **Hook** — the swap itself, under duress, with a clock on it.
- **Beat rhythm** — the substitution → she's set up to fail at something the real bride would have breezed → she succeeds *differently*, on her own terms → the real bride escalates → the groom starts choosing her before he knows why.
- **Cliffhanger** — the substitution about to be exposed; or the real bride returning to reclaim the position.
- **Emotional engine** — the overlooked one being *seen*.
- **Avoid** — a passive stand-in. She must be actively out-playing the situation, not merely enduring it and getting rescued.

## Werewolf / fated mate

- **Premise** — a scorned, rejected heroine turns out to be the fated true mate (the Luna) of the Alpha who cast her out. Supernatural bond vs wounded pride.
- **Hook** — the rejection, public and humiliating, in front of the pack.
- **Beat rhythm** — rejection → the bond asserts itself against both their wills (he feels it, she refuses it) → he begins to grovel while she has *already* moved on → an external threat forces them together → she chooses on her own terms.
- **Cliffhanger** — the bond revealed to the pack; a rival mate arriving; her walking away at the moment he finally kneels.
- **Emotional engine** — "I am not yours to reclaim." The grovel is the product.
- **Avoid** — an instant forgiveness. The bond is a force *she resists*, not a shortcut that resolves the wound.

## Win-back (追妻)

- **Premise** — the one who left is pursued by the one who broke it. Power has inverted and only he hasn't noticed yet.
- **Hook** — the breach or the leaving, at full cost.
- **Beat rhythm** — the breach → she rebuilds without him → escalating pursuit, each attempt costlier and less effective → reversal of who needs whom.
- **Cliffhanger** — a gesture that finally lands (and shouldn't), or a rival arriving to make the pursuit real.
- **Emotional engine** — earned indifference. Her not-caring must be genuine, not a tactic.
- **Avoid** — a heroine who is secretly waiting. If she's waiting, there's no reversal to pay off.
